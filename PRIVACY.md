# Set Back Scoreboard Privacy Policy

Thank you for using **Set Back Scoreboard** ("the App"), an open-source application
designed to help you manage scores while playing the card game, Set Back. This
Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use the App. As
an open-source project, Set Back Scoreboard is not maintained by a commercial
business but by contributors dedicated to providing a simple and fun tool for
players of all ages.

## 1. Information We Collect

**We do not collect, store, or process any personal information about you.**
The App operates entirely on your device, and any data you input (such as player
names or scores) is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to any
external servers or third parties.

- **No Personal Data:** We do not collect identifiers like your name, email,
  phone number, or device ID.
- **No Analytics or Tracking:** The App does not use analytics tools, advertising
  services, or tracking technologies to monitor your usage.
- **Local Data:** Any information you enter (e.g., scores or game details) remains
  on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

## 2. How We Use Information

Since we do not collect any personal information, there is no data for us to use,
share, or process. The App’s sole purpose is to help you keep track of scores during
card games, and all functionality occurs locally on your device.

## 3. Sharing Your Information

We do not share any information with third parties because we do not collect any
data. The App does not transmit data to external services, but it may reach out
to the internet to download dependencies, such as fonts or icons.

## 4. Data Security

As all data entered into the App is stored locally on your device, the security
of that data depends on the security measures you have in place for your device
(e.g., passcodes or biometric locks). Although we feel this app does not include
any sensitive data, we recommend keeping your device secure to protect any
information you input into the App, such as names.

## 5. Children’s Privacy

Set Back Scoreboard is suitable for users of all ages, including children. Since
the App does not collect personal information, it complies with the Children’s
Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations. Parents and guardians
can feel confident that the App does not gather or share any data about its users.

## 6. Open-Source Nature of the App

Set Back Scoreboard is an open-source project, meaning its source code is publicly
available for review and contribution. You can inspect the code to verify that the
App does not collect or transmit personal data. The project is maintained by
volunteers, and no commercial entity is responsible for its operation.

For more details about the source code or to contribute, please visit the [project’s
repository](https://github.com/JagWireZ/setback).

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App’s
functionality or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted within the App
or on the project’s repository page. The effective date at the top of this policy
will be updated accordingly. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

## 8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or the App, please
provide feedback at the Github [issues board](https://github.com/JagWireZ/setback/issues).
As an open-source project, we rely on community feedback to improve and address
concerns.
